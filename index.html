Robotics · Embedded P/01

EcoStrider Hexapod

18-DOF autonomous field robot with live digital twin

Built from scratch using 18 DS3240 servos, ESP32 main controller, dual ESP32-CAMs for stereo vision, dual PCA9685 servo drivers, and a PyQt6 GUI over WebSocket for teleoperation. A Three.js React digital twin mirrors the physical robot in real time with joint sliders, body pose controls, and tripod gait animation. Seven-phase development roadmap through monocular SLAM (PyCuVSLAM), reinforcement learning, and full goal-based autonomy.

7-phase autonomy roadmap · stereo vision · real-time 3D twin

Urban Oasis Intelligence

Multi-layer AI+IoT system — live at AUB

Production system in a real outdoor garden at AUB. Four-layer architecture: agricultural sensors (soil, climate, irrigation), YOLOv5 computer vision cameras for presence and plant monitoring, a central AI reasoning core, and two visitor-facing interactive pedestals. Saves 12.47 kWh/day with 24.25 kg CO₂/day reduction and 11.41% annual ROI. Paper accepted and presented at ICECET 2025.

ESDU Brain

Local-first agentic knowledge system over 25+ years of R&D

Converts ESDU's institutional knowledge into a conversational intelligence layer. Pipeline: Docling ingestion → BAAI/bge-m3 multilingual embeddings → Qdrant vector DB → SQLite metadata → NetworkX knowledge graph → LlamaIndex orchestration → Chainlit UI. Qwen2.5-72B via OpenRouter handles answers; Claude Opus reserved for entity extraction.
