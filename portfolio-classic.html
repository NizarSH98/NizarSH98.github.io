§ 03 / 07 Selected work

Flagship field deployments.

Eight production systems · 2021—2025
Robotics · Embedded systems PROJ.01

EcoStrider — autonomous hexapod robot

18-DOF field robot with a live digital twin

A fully custom hexapod built from scratch: 18 DS3240 servos, ESP32 main controller, dual ESP32-CAMs for stereo vision, dual PCA9685 servo drivers, and a PyQt6 GUI over WebSocket for teleoperation. Three.js React digital twin with confirmed physical dimensions, joint sliders, body pose controls, and tripod gait animation — staged through a 7-phase roadmap toward monocular SLAM, RL, and full goal-based autonomy.

Impact 7-phase autonomy roadmap · stereo vision · live 3D digital twin
AI · IoT · Deployed PROJ.02

Urban Oasis — smart intelligence system

Multi-layer AI+IoT living-lab · live at AUB

Production-deployed system in a real outdoor garden lab at the American University of Beirut. Architecture spans four layers: agricultural sensors (soil, climate, irrigation), YOLOv5 computer-vision cameras for human presence and plant monitoring, a central AI reasoning core, and two visitor-facing interactive pedestals. Saves 12.47 kWh/day with 24.25 kg CO₂/day reduction and 11.41% annual ROI.

YOLOv5ESP32MQTTIoT sensor meshDashboardComputer VisionSolar
Impact 12.47 kWh/day · 24.25 kg CO₂/day ICECET 2025
LLM · Agentic RAG PROJ.03

ESDU Brain — institutional knowledge system

Local-first agentic RAG over 25+ years of R&D

A local-first agentic retrieval system that makes ESDU's quarter-century of institutional knowledge conversationally accessible. Full pipeline: Docling for ingestion, BAAI/bge-m3 multilingual embeddings, Qdrant vector DB, SQLite metadata, NetworkX knowledge graphs, LlamaIndex orchestration, Chainlit chat. Qwen2.5-72B via OpenRouter handles generation; Claude Opus is reserved for entity extraction — all on a single unified API key architecture.

DoclingBAAI/bge-m3QdrantLlamaIndexChainlitNetworkXSQLiteOpenRouter
Impact 25+ years made conversational · multilingual · graph-augmented

VisionFrame — video-to-CV-dataset pipeline

FastAPI + Celery workers on the backend, S3-compatible storage, React / TypeScript annotation workspace on the frontend.
